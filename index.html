Important Safety Precautions

🚦 Road Safety

Always follow traffic signals, wear a helmet while riding and use seat belts in vehicles. Avoid using mobile phones while driving.

🔐 Cyber Safety

Use strong passwords, avoid suspicious links, never share OTPs and protect your personal information online.

🏠 Home Safety

Switch off electrical appliances when not in use, keep emergency contacts available and follow basic fire safety precautions.

🧍 Personal Safety

Stay aware of your surroundings, avoid sharing personal details with strangers and inform trusted people about your travel plans.

🔥 Fire Safety

Know your emergency exits, avoid overloading electrical sockets and follow safe evacuation procedures during a fire.

🏥 Health Safety

Maintain personal hygiene, drink clean water, follow basic first-aid guidance and seek professional medical help when needed.
